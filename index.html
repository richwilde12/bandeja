Bandeja.

Northants padel crew. Find a free court, propose a game, share it to WhatsApp, tap in.

How it works
  1. Find a free court on Playskan
  2. Add it here. You become the booker
  3. Share it to the WhatsApp group
  4. Friends tap the link and press I’m in
  5. When enough are in, you book it and tap I’ve booked it
