BANDEJA. Privacy

Bandeja is a small app for a private group of friends to organise padel games. It is not a commercial service. Last updated 18 November 2026.

What we store

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email address and your name. You also choose a display name. We store your email, display name, and the dates you first and last used the app. We also store the games you create or join, who has paid, and any star ratings and comments you leave.

Where it is stored

All of this is kept in a Google Sheet owned by the person who runs the group. Your device also keeps a copy of the games list so the app works quickly.

Who can see it

Other members of the group see your display name, your games, and your ratings and comments. They never see your email address. The person who runs the group can see the Sheet, which includes email addresses. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we do not do

We do not ask for access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google data. Sign-in is used only to confirm who you are.

Your choices

You can ask the person who runs the group to remove your email, display name and games at any time. You can also remove the app's access in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access.

Cookies and storage

The app stores your sign-in and games in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies of its own.
